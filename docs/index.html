Who this is for

Your company runs on manual effort that should run on systems.

Your teams are good at what they do. It’s the manual work around it that slows them down: the follow-up, the data entry, the reporting, the process only a few people know. As you grow, that work grows with you.

You’ve looked at AI, and some of your people already use it on their own. But scattered tools aren’t a plan, and most of what’s out there is a demo, not a system built around how your company actually works.

i

Too much lives in a few people’s heads.

How work gets done depends on the people who have always done it. When they are out or move on, the work slows down with them.

ii

Manual work is consuming your teams’ week.

Data entry, client follow-up, reporting, scheduling, inbox triage. Work that happens daily in every department, takes real hours, and rarely needs a person’s judgment.

iii

Your systems don’t talk to each other.

Customer records, inbox, accounting, project tools, payments. Each works alone. Together they leave gaps where work falls through and time is lost to handoffs.

iv

You’ve run pilots, but nothing has scaled.

Your teams have tried AI tools, and a few people use them well. But there’s distance between a promising pilot and a system the whole company runs on. That distance is where we work.
