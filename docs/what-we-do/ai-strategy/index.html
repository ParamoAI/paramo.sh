Leadership teams who know AI matters but do not want to spend on tools before they know where they fit.

  • Your team uses AI here and there, but there is no plan behind it.
  • You have tried a pilot or two, and nothing has spread across the company.

  • You want a clear first project, with the hours it should save, before you commit budget.

How it works
  1. 01

    Kickoff

    One hour with you on your goals, where the business feels stuck, and what you have already tried.

  2. 02

    Interview

    Short one-on-ones with the people doing the work, so we see how each job moves from start to finish.

  3. 03

    Map

    We add up the hours each workflow takes every week and what that time costs you.

  4. 04

    Rank

    Each opportunity gets an estimate of hours saved, cost to build and difficulty, sorted into automate now, wait, or leave alone.

  5. 05

    Decide

    We walk your leadership team through the plan and choose the first project together.

What it looks like in practice

A strategy engagement usually turns up a handful of quick wins and one or two bigger projects. A few recent examples:

Accounting firm

Six ranked findings, including no system to flag clients who had gone quiet, and a four-day quick-wins plan the owner can run alone. Projected: about 9 hours a week back.

Wellness brand founder

Four quick-win workflows, and a task that now prepares her for every meeting booked through her scheduling link. Projected: about 5.5 hours a week back.

Storage Advisors, commercial real estate

The assessment found every new inquiry waited on a confidentiality agreement sent by hand. We then automated it on their own Gmail, HubSpot and PandaDoc.

Questions
Do we need to be using AI already?
No. Many companies start here before they have bought anything, so the plan decides which tools are worth paying for.
Who from our team needs to be involved?
One hour with you at the start, short one-on-ones with the people doing the work, and your leadership team for the final walkthrough.
What happens after the plan?
Some teams run the quick wins themselves. Others have us build the first project, train the team, or stay on as a fractional chief AI officer to lead the rollout.
Is this only for large companies?
No. We work with growing businesses, from owner-led teams to companies with several departments. The plan is sized to how your business actually runs.
