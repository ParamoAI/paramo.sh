What we do · Fractional Chief AI Officer

A fractional chief AI officer, without the full-time hire.

We own your AI roadmap alongside your leadership team: choosing tools and vendors, setting the rules, and leading the rollout, part time.

Book a call
Who it is for

Companies that need someone senior to own AI, but not a full-time executive salary.

  • AI is on the leadership agenda every month, and nobody owns it.
  • Vendors keep pitching AI features and you need someone to say which are worth it.
  • You need an AI use policy your team will actually follow.
  • You want progress reviewed on a schedule, not when someone remembers.
What you get

An AI roadmap and priorities

Built from an AI Strategy, kept current as the business changes.

Tool and vendor decisions

We vet software before you buy it, sit in on sales demos and tell you what to skip.

An AI use policy and training

Written for your company, kept current, and taught to the people who use each tool, new hires included.

A monthly leadership meeting

Progress, next priorities, and a quarterly review of what saved time and what did not.

How it works
  1. 01

    Plan

    We start from your AI Strategy. If we have not done one yet, that is the first month.

  2. 02

    Meet

    A standing monthly meeting with your leadership team to review progress and set the next priorities.

  3. 03

    Choose

    We vet software before you buy it, sit in on sales demos and tell you what to skip.

  4. 04

    Roll out

    We write your AI use policy, keep it current, and train the people who will use each new tool, new hires included.

  5. 05

    Review

    Every quarter we look at what saved time, what did not, and set the plan for the next quarter.

Questions
How is this different from AI Strategy?
AI Strategy is a plan. A fractional chief AI officer stays on to lead it: monthly with your leadership team, every quarter for a review.
Do we need an AI Strategy first?
It is the starting point. If we have not done one yet, that is the first month.
Will you replace our IT team or vendors?
No. We work with them, help choose the right tools, and make sure what gets bought actually gets used.
How much of our time does it take?
A standing monthly leadership meeting, plus time with the people rolling out each new tool.
