What we do

Five ways we put AI to work in your business.

Start with one, or combine them. Every engagement is built around how your business already runs.

  1. i

    AI Concierge

    We build it with you, side by side.

    We sit with you, set up Claude for the way you work, and walk you through building the workflows your job needs, step by step, until you are building your own agentic workflows.

    • Claude set up for you
    • Workflows built together
    • Agentic workflows for your role
  2. ii

    Team AI Workshops

    Your whole team, using AI in their real work.

    Hands-on sessions in plain language, built around the tasks your people actually do.

    • Tailored to your tools
  3. iii

    AI Strategy

    A clear plan for what to automate first.

    We map where your hours and money go, then hand you a ranked plan: what to automate now, what can wait, and what to leave alone.

    • Time and workflow audit
    • Ranked opportunities with estimated savings
    • A roadmap you can act on
  4. iv

    Fractional Chief AI Officer

    Senior AI leadership, without a full-time hire.

    We own your AI roadmap alongside your leadership team: choosing tools and vendors, setting the rules, and leading the rollout, part-time.

    • AI roadmap and priorities
    • Tool and vendor decisions
    • AI use policy and training
    • Monthly leadership meeting
  5. v

    Managed Agents

    AI agents that work for you, run by us.

    Agents built around your business that answer, follow up, report and keep work moving. We monitor them, improve them and maintain them so you do not have to.

    • Built on your tools
    • Monitored and maintained
    • Yours to keep
