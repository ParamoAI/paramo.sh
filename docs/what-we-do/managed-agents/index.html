What we do · Managed Agents

AI agents for your small business, run by us.

Agents built around your business that answer, follow up, report and keep work moving. We monitor them, improve them and maintain them so you do not have to.

Book a call
Who it is for

Businesses with repeat work that follows the same steps every time, and no one free to build or babysit an AI system.

  • New inquiries wait because one person handles them by hand.
  • Information gets typed from one system into another.
  • Follow-ups and reports happen when someone remembers.
  • You want it running on your own tools, with a person approving what goes to customers.
What you get

Agents built on your tools

Connected to the email, customer records, accounting and documents you already use.

A person in the loop

Anything a customer sees is drafted for someone on your team to approve, until you choose otherwise.

A simple record

Each run reports what it did, so you can see the work without opening the system.

Monitoring and upkeep

We watch it, fix what breaks, and keep it current as your business changes. It stays yours.

How it works
  1. 01

    Pick

    We choose one job worth handing off first, like answering after-hours calls or chasing overdue invoices.

  2. 02

    Build

    We build the agent on the tools you already use and connect it to the information it needs.

  3. 03

    Test

    It works alongside your team first. Nothing goes out until someone on your side has checked it.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Once it gets the job right, it runs on its own, and you see a simple record of what it did.

  5. 05

    Improve

    We watch it, fix what breaks, keep it current as your business changes, and add the next agent when you are ready.

What it looks like in practice

A few agents we have built and run:

Storage Advisors, commercial real estate

Every new inquiry is matched to the right confidentiality agreement, logged in HubSpot and drafted as a Gmail reply for a person to send. Two weeks after launch the broker was approving replies from his phone.

Junk removal company

Website leads now land in QuickBooks on their own, checked for duplicates, with a report on every run.

Business coaching firm

Every evening at 6pm, the day’s client meetings are summarized and filed in the right client folder.

Questions
Will the agent talk to our customers on its own?
Not at first. It drafts, a person approves. Automatic sending is a later choice, made after weeks of drafts that work.
Where does it run?
On the tools and accounts you already have. Nothing is hidden in a platform you cannot see.
What if something breaks?
Monitoring is part of the service. We fix it, and each run’s report shows you what happened.
Do we own it?
Yes. It is built in your accounts and documented.
