What we do · Team AI Workshops

AI training for your whole team, on their real work.

Hands-on sessions in plain language, built around the tasks your people actually do, so AI becomes part of the job instead of another login.

Book a call
Who it is for

Teams where a few people use AI well and most have not started, or started and stopped.

  • Leadership wants the team using AI, but the team is not sure where it fits their job.
  • People have tried ChatGPT or Claude on their own, with mixed results.
  • You are rolling out a business AI plan and want people to actually use it.
  • You want everyone working from the same habits and the same rules.
What you get

An assessment first

We learn where your team’s hours go and where everyone stands with AI today, so the session fits your people.

A working session on site

90 minutes, a half day or a full day, practiced on your own tools and simple builds, not generic demos.

Done-with-you hours

Time after the session, as a group or one on one, building the workflows your team asked for.

Material to keep

A prompt guide, a simple framework for writing good prompts, and first-week homework.

How a workshop works
  1. 01

    Assess

    We sit with your team, learn where the hours go, and see where everyone stands with AI today.

  2. 02

    Plan

    You get the findings, person by person: where your team’s hours go, what that time is worth, and what to build first.

  3. 03

    Learn

    A working session on site: 90 minutes, a half day or a full day. Your team gets fluent in Claude: projects, skills, artifacts and your own tools connected, practiced on simple builds.

  4. 04

    Build together

    Done-with-you hours, as a group or one on one, building the systems your team asked for so they actually get used.

  5. 05

    Keep going

    Everyone leaves with a prompt guide, a simple framework for writing good prompts, and first-week homework.

What it looks like in practice

Workshops are measured: we survey the team before and after.

SafeRide Health, medical transportation

A paid hands-on Claude workshop for the account management team. Clarity on where AI could help rose from 2.3 to 4.2 out of 5, and confidence using it from 2.5 to 3.7.

Free lunch and learns

About eight so far across five Twin Cities coworking spaces, with rooms of 5 to 30 people. The SafeRide Health workshop came out of one of them.

Questions
Does our team need to be technical?
No. Everything is in plain language and practiced on the work people already do.
Which AI tools do you teach?
Mostly Claude and ChatGPT on business plans, connected to the tools your team already uses. If you already pay for Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, we start there.
Is it in person or remote?
The working session is on site. Follow-up hours can be in person or remote.
What happens after the workshop?
The done-with-you hours turn what people learned into workflows they use every week. Some teams then add an AI Strategy or a fractional chief AI officer.
