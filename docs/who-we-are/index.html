Our roots

Rooted in Colombia. Named for its high ground.

Páramo was founded by Dennis and Adrian, two Colombians raised in households that taught them the same things: work hard, take care of family, and make a lot from a little.

When it came time to name the company, they wanted something that stood for the peak, the highest point of what technology can do. They found it in the páramo, the misty highland above the Andean cloud forest, and one of Colombia’s most remarkable places.

The more they learned about how a páramo works, the more it shaped how Páramo works. Its signature plant, the frailejón, pulls water out of the passing fog and releases it slowly into the streams below. Much of Bogotá’s (the capital of Colombia) water starts there.

Páramo catches the work already slipping through the week, shapes it into something reliable, and lets it run steadily on its own.
